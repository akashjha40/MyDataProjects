**Ad Campaign Data Analysis**



**Python EDA + Power BI Dashboard**



**Overview**



**This project analyzes digital ad campaign data to understand what is actually happening across impressions, clicks, conversions, spend, and profit. The focus is on turning raw marketing data into clear business insights.**



**Python was used for data exploration and understanding, while Power BI was used to build an interactive dashboard with advanced DAX measures.**



**Tools Used**



**Python (Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn)**



**Power BI (Data modeling, DAX, Dashboarding)**



**What Was Done**



**Python (EDA):**



**Cleaned and explored the data**



**Analyzed distributions and outliers**



**Compared platform-wise performance**



**Studied relationships between spend, clicks, conversions, and profit**



**Power BI:**



**Built KPI cards for impressions, clicks, conversions, spend, and profit**



**Created complex DAX measures like CTR, conversion rate, ROAS, cost per conversion, and profit per conversion**



**Visualized trends over time and platform-wise performance**



**Questions Answered**



**Which platforms are actually profitable?**



**Are high impressions leading to real conversions?**



**Where is the ad spend being wasted?**



**Which days and months perform better?**



**How efficient is each platform in terms of cost and return?**



**Key Insights**



**High impressions do not always mean high profit**



**A few platforms drive most of the conversions and revenue**



**CTR and conversion performance varies by day and platform**



**ROAS trends help identify underperforming periods**



**Why This Project**



**This project shows an end-to-end marketing analytics workflow, from raw data analysis to business-focused insights using Python and Power BI.**
